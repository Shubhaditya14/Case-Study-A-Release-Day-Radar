# Phase 1 check-in — Release Day Radar

**Question:** Did trailer-week YouTube engagement predict first-weekend collections for 10 Hindi and regional films released in 2025–26, and does audience sentiment add anything beyond raw views? **Decision it serves:** the Head of Distribution's call, 72 hours after each Friday release, on whether to add screens, cut screens or spend more on promotion. **Films (10):** Chhaava, Saiyaara, Sitaare Zameen Par, Housefull 5, Raid 2 and Dhurandhar: The Revenge (Hindi), plus Kantara: Chapter 1 (Kannada), Lokah Chapter 1: Chandra and L2: Empuraan (Malayalam) and Peddi (Telugu). They were chosen from 18 candidates because each has a first-weekend figure on Wikipedia and an official YouTube trailer with comments open. **Sources confirmed working on 2026-11-26:** (1) Wikipedia through the MediaWiki API, which gives HTML box-office tables and text inside JSON; (2) TMDb API (JSON), which gives genre, runtime and language; (3) YouTube Data API v3 (JSON), which gives trailer stats and comment text; (4) Google Trends daily interest in India, exported as CSV by hand; and (5) the IMDb 50K labelled reviews (CSV), used to validate the sentiment method. **Early lesson:** Wikipedia reports opening figures inconsistently (worldwide vs India-only, 3-day vs 4-day weekends, ₹ ranges vs USD), so our outcome is first-weekend worldwide gross divided by the number of weekend days, and every exception is logged in the cleaning log.
